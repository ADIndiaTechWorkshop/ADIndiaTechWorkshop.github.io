10 October 2026

What we covered

  1. 2:30-3:00 pm

    Arrivals and welcome

  2. 3:00-4:00 pm

    Session 1. Spec-driven development

    Priyam Gupta and Shashwat Rawat used a Digital Commerce Experience scenario to show how a product spec, a visual spec, agent orchestration, and implementation fit into one build workflow.

  3. 4:00-5:00 pm

    Session 2. Efficient AI engineering

    Yash Sinha and Pratikkumar Mohite covered tighter context, reusable capabilities, smaller token use, phased work, and routines for tasks that repeat.

  4. 5:00-5:30 pm

    High tea and open discussion

  5. 5:30-6:15 pm

    Phase 3. Devs.ai and App Builder

    Uday Girhepunje walked through the platform and a short App Builder demo: what it includes, how teams at AppDirect use it, and when App Builder is the right path versus a spec-driven engineering build.

  6. 6:15-6:30 pm

    Closing and feedback
